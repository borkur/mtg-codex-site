Support

Found a bug, have a question or an idea? Open an issue on GitHub. Please include your device, iOS version and what you were doing. Do not post anything private.

Common questions

Search says it can't reach Scryfall. Check your connection, or download card data in More → Offline to search without a network.

Why do prices look out of date? Prices are a snapshot from Scryfall and refresh when you view a card or pull to refresh your collection.

Where is my data? Only on your device. Export a CSV from the Collection tab to back it up.

I don't want sounds or haptics. Both are off or switchable in the More tab.
